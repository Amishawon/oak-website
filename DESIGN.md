# Oak visual system

The public website follows the supplied Therapy by Grace reference, adapted to Oak's existing logo, content, routes, photography, and CMS controls. The homepage is a Persuade surface: a pale blue hero with portrait, three care links, a wide session image, an introduction, stacked service rows, existing supporting sections, and a forest green appointment call to action and footer.

Tokens: forest/action default #084f3c (CMS primary color remains supported), pale blue #eaf4f6, mint #a9e4dc, muted green text #49665d, white canvas. Retain Oak's pink accent for focus states. Georgia serif headings pair with bundled Poppins body text. Headings use tracking no tighter than -.035em. Buttons have 6px radii; photography and service rows use 14–16px radii.

At mobile widths the care links become compact rows and service photography stacks beneath its copy. Preserve accessible menu controls, routes, CMS section order and visibility, approved-content filtering, draft previews, and publishing behavior. Existing service images may repeat according to CMS data. The admin remains an Operate surface with its existing styling.
